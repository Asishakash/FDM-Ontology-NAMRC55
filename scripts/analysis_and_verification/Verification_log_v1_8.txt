FDM quality ontology v1.8 - verification log (27 Oct 2026)
==========================================================

1. Why v1.8
 Your Protégé export of v1.7 (inferred axioms) classified all 27 setting evaluations correctly. With the asserted
 recommendation types removed and two requirements added at once, Pellet 2.3.1 left the new assessments without
 outcomes in 5 of 10 runs (deterministic per statement order: Python hash seeds 1 and 2 failed), while reporting the
 ontology consistent. The delivered v1.7 file was complete in all 16 orders tried. Cause: the nominal closure
 "coversAssessment only {a1, a2}" combined with the SWRL rules - the same family of Pellet incompleteness as in v1.5.

2. What changed (all data, limits and rules R1-R7 unchanged)
 - Removed: the nominal closure on every SettingEvaluation; the defined-class equivalences of ProcessRecommendation
   and NotRecommendedSetting.
 - Added: coversAssessment max 2 on every SettingEvaluation (exactly two coupons were printed per setting) and the
   class TwoReplicateEvaluation.
 - Added rules:
   R8_RecommendAllReplicates: TwoReplicateEvaluation(?e), coversAssessment(?e, ?a1), coversAssessment(?e, ?a2),
     differentFrom(?a1, ?a2), hasOutcome(?a1, Acceptable_Instance), hasOutcome(?a2, Acceptable_Instance)
     -> ProcessRecommendation(?e)
   R9_NotRecommended: SettingEvaluation(?e), coversAssessment(?e, ?a), hasOutcome(?a, Defective_Instance)
     -> NotRecommendedSetting(?e)
   ProcessRecommendation and NotRecommendedSetting stay disjoint. The assessments of each evaluation are declared
   different, so a third covered assessment violates max 2 (negative test 2).
 - Scope: R8 is written for two replicates; fdm_dss.add_requirement refuses other replicate counts (an n-replicate
   design needs its own class and rule).
 - Final labels: ontology label 'FDM Process-Parameter-Based Quality Classification and Decision Support Ontology';
   ProcessParameter subclass PrintSpeed renamed FirstLayerSpeed; material label 'PLA (Overture, 1.75 mm)' for all sets.
 - Metrics: 28 classes, 17 object properties, 22 data properties, 11 SWRL rules, 139 individuals.
